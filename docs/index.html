Type a question the way you would say it. Blink reads your Search Console data and answers with the figures, a chart, and what to look at next.

  • A report worth sending Ask for a report, preview the PDF, and share it in one tap.
  • He keeps watch Alerts arrive only when clicks really move, with the figures. Tap one and he shows you what changed.

What is inside

Every screen answers a real question

Not a wall of menus. Each view maps to what you already ask in Search Console, sized for a quick read on iOS. When you need to share, export CSV or download a custom PDF report.

  • Did traffic move?

    Clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position without digging through tabs.

  • Which queries and pages shifted?

    See what gained, what slipped, and what is newly visible.

  • Is the site still healthy?

    Core Web Vitals beside your traffic, not in a separate tool.

  • Need to share or save the numbers?

    Export results to CSV for your spreadsheet, or download a custom PDF report ready to send.

  • Did something change overnight?

    Alerts when performance moves enough to matter.

  • Where is attention going?

    Spot pages pulling clicks and pages quietly losing ground.
