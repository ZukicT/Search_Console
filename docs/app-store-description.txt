Search Console 2.5 puts your Google Search Console data in a fast native iPhone app, and adds Blink: an on-device assistant that answers questions about your own search traffic. This is an independent app. It is not made by Google.

MEET BLINK
• Ask in your own words: "Why did traffic drop?" Blink reads your Search Console data and answers with the figures, a chart and what to look at next.
• Nothing made up: Every figure comes from your data. Blink tells you what changed and says so when the data cannot explain why.
• Private: Blink runs on your device. Your questions do not leave your iPhone. Requires a device with Apple Intelligence.
• Reports: Ask for a report, preview the PDF and share it in one tap.
• Switch sites: Type @ to pick a property, or compare two.

KEY FEATURES
• Overview: Clicks, impressions, CTR and average position with the change against the previous period, and a chart you can scrub.
• Search queries and top pages: See what drives traffic; sort by clicks, CTR or position.
• Core Web Vitals: Test LCP, CLS and INP on mobile and desktop.
• URL Inspection: See how Google indexes a URL.
• Sitemaps: View submitted sitemaps and their status.
• Integrations: Link Google Analytics and AdMob to a site to see visitors and revenue alongside search.
• Widgets: Small, medium and large home screen widgets.

ALERTS THAT MEAN SOMETHING
• Performance alerts arrive only when clicks really move, with the figures.
• Daily, weekly or monthly summaries carry your latest numbers.
• Tap an alert and Blink shows you what changed.

A NEW LOOK
Version 2.5 is redesigned from top to bottom: a clean white interface, clear tables and charts, and motion and sound that tell you what just happened.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
Sign in with Google using OAuth. The app talks directly to Google's Search Console API. We do not store or sell your Search Console data.

PLANS
• Free with ads: One property.
• Pro: All properties, Blink, no ads, Core Web Vitals and URL Inspection. $5.99 per month with a 3-day free trial, or $1.99 per week. Cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.

GET STARTED
Download Search Console, sign in with your Google account, pick a property and ask Blink your first question.
